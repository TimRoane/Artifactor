# Interpretation guide

`separable` means configured variables have sufficient independent support for evaluation. `weak_overlap` means correction may proceed with caution. `highly_confounded` indicates strong alignment. `non_identifiable` blocks correction when protected biology cannot be separated from technical handling.

Biological, technical, mixed, and unexplained labels summarize observed associations only. Always inspect effect size, uncertainty, overlap, and feature evidence. Validate a technical hypothesis by rerunning bridge samples across lots, batches, operators, or plate positions under blinded and randomized conditions.
